The extended version of <a href="https://joris1724.github.io/vzome-sharing/2026/05/26/Robert-Webb-Toroid-11-58-15.html">Robert Webb's Genus-41 Toroid</a> is large enough to encompass <a href="https://joris1724.github.io/vzome-sharing/2026/08/20/Bonnie-Stewart-Toroids-22-23-25.html">Bonnie Stewart's Holey Monster</a> and a spherical layer of Triangular Cupola's (J3's). If one could connect each triangular face of the inner boundary of the Genus-41 to a hexagonal face of the outer boundary <i>Holey Monster</i> through a J3, than the Genus could be extended to a whopping 106.

The second scene of the vZome model shows a <i>Holey Monster</i> (green) and a antipodal set of a J3's (purple) which are supposed to connect to the Genus-41 toroid. Of the latter, only two J63 fragments (red) are shown.

Unfortunately, the J3's height is about 1/8 to short to make contact with both toroids. The only way to salvage the problem is shown in the third scene: shift the <i>Holey Monster</i> a bit so that at least one of the twenty tunnels can be made. This result in a Genus-87 toroid:
